# Contributing

Thanks for looking. This is an early, open research project and help of any size counts.

## Ways to help

1. **Review the method.** Read [`METHODS.md`](METHODS.md) and open an issue with anything that looks wrong or missing. This is the most useful thing right now.
2. **Run a measurement on your hardware.** Once the harness lands, one command will run the benchmark on your GPU and produce a result file. Different GPUs make the dataset more useful.
3. **Pick up an issue.** Look for the `good first issue` label.

## Credit

- Everyone who contributes a measurement run, code, or a substantive method review is credited in the dataset and any write-up.
- Substantial contributors (e.g. owning an experiment or a major component) are offered co-authorship on resulting papers.

## Ground rules

- Every number must be reproducible from a committed command and raw log.
- Keep pull requests small and focused.
